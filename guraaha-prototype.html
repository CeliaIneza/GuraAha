How GuraAha works

From search to handshake in four steps

1

Search by location

Drill down Province → District → Sector → Cell → Village to find land where you want it.

2

View full details

Photos, size, UPI number, price and the neighbourhood's investment highlights — all free.

3

Unlock contact via MoMo

Pay 100 RWF for the agent's number or 200 RWF for the owner's — instantly by mobile money.

4

Meet & buy safely

Every agent is identity-verified with a unique code. Certified surveyors & notaries in our directory.

🎉

Monthly GuraAha Lotto

Every contact unlock = one free entry. This month's prize: 250,000 RWF — drawn live on the 30th.

📞

No smartphone? No problem.

District representatives and nearby blockers assist buyers and sellers without smartphones — by phone call or in person. GuraAha works for everyone.
